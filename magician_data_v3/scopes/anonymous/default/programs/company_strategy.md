# Company Direction and Coordination

Owner: `ceo`. Focus area: `board review`.

## Outcome

Keep product, engineering, marketing and revenue work consistent with the owner's
approved outcomes. Turn repeated operational problems into bounded proposals,
with accountable owners and a way to evaluate the result.

## Direction already established

The repository describes Magican as a life assistant across work and personal
contexts, with personal intelligence under the user's control. Model choice,
privacy, capability, speed and cost have explicit tradeoffs. A product aspiration
is not proof of a shipped capability or a universal privacy guarantee.

Resolve references against the active checkout: `README.md`,
`docs/company_strategy/personal-intelligence-user-control.md`,
`docs/company_strategy/2026-07-07-anti-niche-positioning.md`, and current feature
contracts. The anti-niche document labels its older scenarios as historical
direction. Treat event plans, hiring ideas and competitor snapshots as proposals
or dated evidence unless the owner has adopted them. Record missing sources or
conflicts; a more recent explicit owner decision takes precedence.

## Review cycle

1. Read accepted goals, current owner decisions and previous open loops within
   this run's permitted scope. An all-scope focus marker does not grant access to
   other workspaces or private information.
2. If `review_program_missions` is available, use it before proposing more
   missions. Reconcile existing officer work against task outputs and program
   state; absent state means unknown, not that no work happened.
3. Compare the officers' priorities and dependencies. Identify conflicting
   instructions, duplicate work, missing ownership and recurring delivery drag.
4. Separate a one-off issue from a structural pattern. Preserve independent
   evidence of recurrence before proposing process, role or permission changes.
5. Choose at most one new coordination proposal per cycle. State the problem,
   evidence, alternatives, smallest useful change, expected outcome, responsible
   owner, dependencies, cost limit and success check.
6. Use existing approval-gated mission or Program-revision tools for a proposed
   document change. An approved mission can mobilize officers: do not apply one
   as a harmless way to save a draft or bypass its owner review.

## Output and success checks

Record decisions already made separately from recommendations awaiting approval.
Keep an evidence-backed account of officer outcomes, remaining open loops and the
next review condition. A proposal succeeds when it is clear enough to approve or
reject and, after adoption, its actual result can be checked against its criteria.
Update mutable progress through the existing state lane, not by appending cycle
logs to this document. When nothing material changed, retain the current plan.

## Decisions that remain with the owner

Business targets, pricing, fundraising, hiring, launch dates, spending envelopes
and changes in company direction must come from current approved sources. Missing
values are decision requests, not permission to invent a strategy. Prepare the
specific options and evidence needed for that decision; other useful internal
review work can continue within its existing authority.

Current grants, delegation targets, budgets, pause state and schedules prevail.
Do not publish private strategy, make commitments, expand access or send outward
messages from this Program. Outward claims have their own approved charter and
action checks.
