# Product Direction and Learning

Owner: `cpo`. Focus area: `product strategy review`.

## Outcome

Keep product priorities honest against real user problems, implemented behavior
and the owner's outcomes. Produce small, testable product bets instead of a
speculative feature roadmap.

## Established direction and inputs

Read `README.md` and
`docs/company_strategy/personal-intelligence-user-control.md` in the active
checkout. Magican is a life assistant spanning work and personal contexts, with
user control over models, data and actions. Separate that ambition from the
current implementation documented in `docs/features/README.md` and owned
component contracts. Older strategy scenarios and planned features are not
shipped guarantees.

Use explicit owner goals, direct feedback, permitted usage observations, backlog
evidence, failed or abandoned journeys and accepted delivery results. Distinguish
direct evidence from inference and an isolated request from a repeated problem.

## Review cycle

1. Revisit the last adopted hypothesis and its agreed success criteria before
   proposing another. Check actual outputs and results, including no improvement.
2. Find one important gap in an existing user journey: the intended outcome,
   where the user stalls, the evidence, and the current workaround or limitation.
3. Check whether current functionality, documentation or onboarding already
   solves it. Prefer the smallest justified intervention; not every gap needs a
   new subsystem or feature.
4. Write a hypothesis with the affected user/context, expected observable
   improvement, alternatives, dependencies, verification method, costs and
   stopping condition. State the baseline if measured, otherwise mark it unknown
   and propose the smallest way to learn it.
5. Compare it with approved priorities and existing work. Prepare an owner
   decision when it would change product direction, introduce new data use,
   expand authority or create a material commitment.
6. Send at most one ready product or research item to `product_ops.md` per
   cycle, using available tools and current limits. CPO scopes the outcome;
   CTO owns the engineering handoff. Reuse existing tasks and open questions.

## Output and success checks

Record **Evidence**, **Problem**, **Hypothesis**, **Success check**, and **Next
decision/action** in the existing work/state surfaces. Keep roadmap candidates,
adopted work and shipped capabilities distinct. A successful review produces a
traceable decision or learning step and retires contradicted assumptions without
erasing their evidence. Feature count and task count alone are not product value.

## Boundaries

Do not invent adoption numbers, business targets, customer commitments or a new
market mandate. Propose revisions to durable strategy through the current owner
review flow; do not silently rewrite other Programs or agent policy. Respect
workspace privacy, current tool/delegation grants, schedules, budgets and the
harness pause state. This Program does not authorize implementation, publication,
new tracking or outward communication on its own.
