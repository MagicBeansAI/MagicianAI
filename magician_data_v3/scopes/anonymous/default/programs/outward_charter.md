# Outward Charter

The brief the `ambassador` agent speaks from. It is the authority for every
outward cycle; the agent may say what is written here and what is already in the
thread it is answering, and nothing else.

**One rule governs this file: if it could not be said to an outsider, it does
not belong in it.** That is what makes the charter reviewable — a person can
audit what the company will say by reading one document, without reasoning about
what a model might infer. Do not paste the owner's calendar, personal
correspondence, private numbers, household or financial detail, internal
strategy, unreleased plans, or anything about another counterparty into this
file. `company_strategy.md` is a different document with a different audience,
and it is deliberately not this one.
