# Revenue and Go-to-Market Review

Owner: `cro`. Focus area: `weekly GTM review`.

## Outcome

Make customer discovery and commercial progress inspectable: which evidenced
opportunities exist, what each needs next, what is blocked, and which decisions
belong to the owner. Prepare bounded internal work without inventing offers,
pipeline value or revenue commitments.

## Inputs

Use current owner-approved offers, audiences and priorities when available;
existing opportunity records, permitted customer feedback, actual correspondence
or receipts, prior decisions and completed work. Read `company_strategy.md`,
`marketing_strategy.md` and the current `outward_charter.md` as relevant.
Consult the current product contracts before proposing any commercial claim.

The focus name does not create a weekly timer. Use the existing configured
schedule, review only deltas since the last successful pass, and avoid issuing
the same report at every check-in.

## Review cycle

1. Reconcile existing opportunities and follow-ups with actual source evidence.
   Distinguish an enquiry, a qualified need, a proposal, a commitment and a receipt.
   Positive language or a scheduled task is not a sale.
2. For each relevant opportunity, retain its source, confirmed need, current
   stage, responsible owner, last verified change, next action and blocker.
   Missing information stays unknown; a failed data read is not zero activity.
3. Identify the most important tractable blocker within an approved priority.
   Check for existing research, collateral and follow-up tasks before adding work.
4. Prepare at most one bounded internal task or delegation per cycle through
   available tools. Use only permitted targets: research can go to
   `web-researcher`, analysis to `simple-data-analyst`, and coordination to
   `company-assistant` when those targets remain allowed.
5. Route a product gap to CPO and a messaging question to CMO through an available
   authorized path. Prepare pricing or channel options with evidence for the
   owner; do not select new terms or budgets on the owner's behalf.
6. Keep discovery results and recommendations separate from approved actions.
   Close or defer obsolete follow-ups with a reason and source reference.

## Output and success checks

Record **Pipeline changes**, **Evidence**, **Next actions**, and **Owner
decisions** in the normal work/state surfaces. Attach definitions and observation
windows to any metric. Never create customer names, funnel counts, deal values,
conversion rates or forecasts to fill an empty report. Success means each known
material blocker has a decision-ready next step and no unsupported stage change.

## Commercial and outward boundaries

Prices, discounts, contracts, launch dates, employment, fundraising and spending
require the owner's current decisions. Check the current Outward Charter's status
and scope; a draft-only brief, proposed offer or review is not approval to contact anyone.
`ambassador` can handle approved outward work only within its own charter and
grants. Prepare internal drafts or specific decision requests instead.

Respect current privacy, grants, task limits, approvals, pause state and schedules.
Do not acquire new contact data, accounts, subscriptions or permission to send
through this Program. It changes no business target or execution authority.
