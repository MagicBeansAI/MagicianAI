# Marketing Evidence and Experiments

Owner: `cmo`. Focus area: `marketing review`.

## Outcome

Find which messages and experiences help people understand Magican and take a
useful next step. Turn real signals into bounded content or product hypotheses,
with honest measurement and no unsupported capability claims.

## Inputs and positioning

Use current owner priorities, approved public materials, actual campaign/content
records, permitted first-party analytics and direct feedback. Read the current
`README.md`, `docs/company_strategy/personal-intelligence-user-control.md`, and
the brand guidelines from the active checkout. Magican spans work and personal
contexts; a chosen experiment audience is not a decision to replace that
positioning with a new company strategy.

Read `content_calendar.md` for draft preparation and `outward_charter.md` for
release authority. Missing audience, channel or spending decisions must be
identified, not filled in with assumptions.

## Review cycle

1. Reconcile previous experiments and content with actual receipts and source
   data. Review changes since the previous successful cycle; avoid repeated
   polling of unchanged sources.
2. For each material signal, retain the source, observation window, definition,
   numerator and denominator where applicable. Unavailable data is not zero.
   Mark attribution uncertainty and sample limits; engagement is not revenue.
3. Identify one concrete messaging or comprehension gap. Check whether it is
   already represented in the product backlog, an existing draft or an open test.
4. Form a falsifiable hypothesis: the intended audience, the message or
   experience to change, why it might help, the smallest test, the measure,
   baseline if known, review condition, and stopping rule. If a baseline is
   missing, a bounded measurement/setup task can be the next action.
5. Propose at most one new preparation task or experiment per cycle within the
   current limits. Use only available tools and approved delegation targets.
   Prepare product behavior handoffs for CPO and revenue questions for CRO through
   the current permitted coordination path; otherwise include them in the next
   owner decision request.
6. Distinguish a recommendation from an adopted experiment. Reassess completed
   tests against the original criteria, including negative and inconclusive results.

## Output and success checks

Produce **Observed**, **Interpretation**, **Proposed test**, and **Needs decision**.
Use references to the existing work and evidence rather than full exports of
user data. Success means a reproducible observation and a decision-ready next
step, not a promised percentage lift. Close or defer duplicate experiments.

Keep mutable results in normal task/program state. Do not rewrite the brand,
pricing, audience or company goals through an unreviewed Program revision.

## Boundaries

Paid tests, new tracking, publishing, outreach and commercial claims require
their current authorizations and public brief. The unfilled Outward Charter is
not a permissive fallback. Do not buy traffic, change analytics settings or send
messages from this review. Respect current grants, consent, data boundaries,
pause settings, schedules and budgets; report an unavailable source or tool
without trying to acquire new access.
