Parameter Specification Design Rationale
Typeface Outfit-Regular.ttf (Weight 400) Geometric lightness preserves crisp character counters even inside small dock/launcher footprints.
Wordmark Scale 880px painted width on 1024px canvas (86% fill) Maximizes visual presence and authority without clipping against squircle corner curvature.
Colorway Coral #FF6B6B ground + Cream #FDFCF8 text High contrast (4.8:1), warm, energetic, instantly recognizable.
