## Brand philosophy (from the author, 2026-10-03)

| | |
|---|---|
| Who am I? | **magican** |
| What personality do I have? | **⭐ the star**: a nerdy little sidekick |
| Why do I exist? | **More You** |
| What am I? | **YOUR PERSONAL SUPERPOWERS.** |
| What does having me feel like? | **SUPERPOWERS FOR WORK, PLAY AND EVERYTHING IN BETWEEN.** |

Magican isn't the superhero. You are. Magican gives you your personal superpowers; the result
is more you.

The star always carries its companion: a small black dot floating off its upper-right point
with three short dash lines (glowing yellow on dark backgrounds). Every star shot includes it.
On end cards the star is centre and upfront, never peeking from an edge.

The star is the sidekick, never the superhero: ridiculously capable, slightly awkward, happy
to stay in the background while you become the star. In every shot the people are the
heroes (capes are theirs, not the star's); the star helps, carries, waves, and steps aside.

The wordmark and app type are Outfit (Google Fonts). Lines like "More You" are set in
type, never handwritten. "More You" is always title case (no full stop) and never larger than
the magican wordmark.
